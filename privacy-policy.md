Moodrot Privacy Policy

Moodrot does not collect personal information such as names, emails, passwords, payment information or private messages.

The extension stores reaction-related data and extension settings required for the visual reaction system and community overlays.

Moodrot may process visible website content on supported websites in order to display custom reactions and overlays.

No personal data is sold or shared with third parties.

Firebase may be used to synchronize public reaction counts and community reaction data.

Users can remove the extension at any time to stop all functionality.
